# Destination: Maldives

## Category: Visa
Indian citizens receive a free 30-day Tourist Visa on Arrival at Velana International Airport (Male). Requirements include a passport valid for at least six months from the arrival date, a confirmed hotel or resort booking confirmation (or proof of sufficient funds of 100 USD + 50 USD per day), and a confirmed onward/return flight ticket. All travelers must submit the online IMUGA Traveller Declaration form within 96 hours before their arrival and departure flights.

## Category: Transport
Transportation in the Maldives depends on the destination island's distance from Male. Near-atoll resorts and local inhabited islands (e.g., Maafushi, Hulhumale) are accessible via speedboat transfers (20 to 60 minutes) or public MTCC dhoni ferries. Remote luxury resorts require scenic domestic seaplane flights operated by Trans Maldivian Airways or Flyme. Within the capital island of Male and Hulhumale, taxis have regulated flat fares, and the Sinamale China-Maldives Friendship Bridge enables quick overland taxi transfers between the airport island and Male city.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The official currency is the Maldivian Rufiyaa (MVR), though the US Dollar (USD) is universally accepted and often used as the primary billing currency in resorts and private excursions. Major international credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) are accepted at all resort properties, dive centers, and high-end restaurants. If staying on local inhabited islands (guesthouses), carrying small USD notes or MVR cash is recommended for local eateries and small grocery stores. Note that resorts add a mandatory 16% TGST (Tourism Goods and Services Tax) and 10% service charge to bills.

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
The Maldives is a 100% Islamic republic, and regulations differ significantly between private luxury resort islands and local inhabited islands. On local residential islands, alcohol and pork are strictly illegal, and modest attire is required (bikinis and swimwear are restricted exclusively to marked 'Bikini Beaches'). On private resort islands, international resort wear, swimwear, and alcohol consumption are fully permitted. Remove footwear when entering local Maldivian homes or mosques.

## Category: Safety And Scams
The Maldives is exceptionally safe with negligible crime on resort islands and low crime in urban centers. Marine safety is the primary consideration: always heed resort flag warnings regarding ocean currents, wear life jackets during snorkeling excursions, and never touch fragile coral reefs or marine life. Ensure speedboats or dive boats are properly licensed and carry safety gear.
