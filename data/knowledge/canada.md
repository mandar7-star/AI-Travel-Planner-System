# Destination: Canada

## Category: Visa
Indian passport holders require a Temporary Resident Visa (Visitor Visa / TRV) to visit Canada for tourism. Applications are submitted online via the IRCC (Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada) portal followed by biometrics enrollment at a VFS Global center in India. The visa application fee is 100 CAD plus an 85 CAD biometrics fee. Indian citizens who hold a valid US non-immigrant visa or have held a Canadian visa in the past 10 years may be eligible to apply for an Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA) when arriving in Canada by air under the expanded eTA program.

## Category: Transport
Covering long distances between major Canadian cities (e.g., Vancouver to Calgary, Toronto to Montreal) is fastest via domestic flights operated by Air Canada, WestJet, or Porter Airlines. For regional journeys in the Quebec City–Windsor Corridor, VIA Rail provides comfortable, scenic passenger train services. In urban hubs like Toronto (TTC subway and streetcars) and Vancouver (TransLink SkyTrain), tap-to-pay using contactless credit cards or transit passes (Presto / Compass card) makes navigating simple. Uber and Lyft operate extensively in all major cities.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The currency is the Canadian Dollar (CAD / $). Canada is an overwhelmingly cashless society where contactless credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay are accepted virtually everywhere, from food markets to national park gatehouses. Carrying more than 20 to 50 CAD in physical cash is rarely necessary. Tipping is customary across Canada, standard at 15% to 20% in sit-down restaurants, 1 to 2 CAD per coat/bag, and 10% to 15% for taxi drivers and tour guides.

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
Canadian culture is known globally for politeness, multicultural tolerance, and deep respect for public order. Apologizing by saying 'sorry' is a reflexive social habit in minor everyday interactions. In the French-speaking province of Quebec (Montreal, Quebec City), greeting staff with 'Bonjour' is polite and warmly received. Remember that retail prices in Canada do not include federal (GST) and provincial (PST/HST) sales taxes, which are calculated and added at final checkout.

## Category: Safety And Scams
Canada is consistently ranked as one of the safest countries in the world with very low crime rates. Basic urban precautions should be observed in small downtown pockets of major cities (such as Vancouver's Downtown Eastside or parts of downtown Toronto late at night). In natural mountain parks like Banff, Jasper, and Whistler, wildlife safety is essential: store food securely away from wildlife, carry bear spray on wilderness trails, maintain a safe distance from elk and bears, and never feed wild animals.
