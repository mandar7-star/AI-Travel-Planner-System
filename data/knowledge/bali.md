# Destination: Bali

## Category: Visa
Indian citizens visiting Bali (Indonesia) can obtain a 30-day Visa on Arrival (e-VOA or physical VOA at Ngurah Rai Airport / DPS). The cost is 500,000 IDR (approx. USD 35 / INR 2,900) and can be extended once for an additional 30 days. Requirements include a passport valid for at least 6 months from entry date and a confirmed return/onward flight ticket. Additionally, complete the electronic customs declaration (e-CD) and pay the Bali Tourist Tax (150,000 IDR) online before arrival.

## Category: Transport
Bali does not have a comprehensive train or public bus system. The primary transportation modes are private driver rentals (approx. INR 2,500 - 4,000 per 10-hour day including fuel and driver), scooter rentals (approx. INR 400 - 700 per day with international driving permit required), or ride-hailing via Grab and Gojek apps (note that local taxi cooperatives restrict ride-hailing pickups in certain zones like central Ubud and Canggu).

## Category: Culture and Etiquette
Balinese Hinduism involves daily offerings (Canang Sari) placed on sidewalks and temple entrances—never step on or kick them intentionally. When visiting temples (like Uluwatu, Tanah Lot, Besakih), both men and women must wear a sarong and sash around the waist (available for rent at temple gates). Never touch a local person's head, and avoid using the left hand when handing money or food.
