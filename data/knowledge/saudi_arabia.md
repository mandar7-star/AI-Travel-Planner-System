# Destination: Saudi Arabia

## Category: Visa
Indian passport holders holding a valid, used tourist or business visa from the US, UK, or Schengen Area (or permanent residence in the US, UK, EU, or GCC) can obtain an instant Saudi Tourist Visa on Arrival or apply online via the official eVisa portal (visa.visitsaudi.com). Indian citizens without qualifying visas can apply for a standard tourist visa through authorized Tasheer visa service centers in India, or take advantage of the free 96-hour Saudi Stopover Transit Visa when flying with Saudia or Flynas. The tourist eVisa fee is approx. 395 SAR (including mandatory health insurance).

## Category: Transport
Saudi Arabia has rapidly modernized its national transport infrastructure. The Haramain High-Speed Railway connects Medina, King Abdullah Economic City (KAEC), Jeddah, and Mecca at speeds up to 300 km/h in total luxury. In Riyadh, the cutting-edge Riyadh Metro network provides fast connections across major districts, while the Careem and Uber ride-hailing apps provide the standard means of urban transportation. For intercity travel to heritage regions like AlUla or the Red Sea resorts, Saudia and Flynas operate frequent domestic flights.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The currency is the Saudi Riyal (SAR), pegged to the US Dollar at roughly 3.75 SAR per USD. Saudi Arabia has one of the highest card payment adoption rates globally (under the national 'Mada' payment system), with contactless credit and debit cards accepted everywhere from large luxury malls to small roadside grocery stalls and dates markets. Carrying a modest amount of cash (50 to 100 SAR) is useful for small tips, private driver gratuities, and traditional heritage souks in old Diriyah or historic Jeddah (Al-Balad).

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
Saudi Arabia is the spiritual birthplace of Islam and is undergoing a major cultural opening under Vision 2030. Visitors should dress respectfully in public spaces (smart-casual attire covering shoulders and knees; abayas and head coverings are no longer legally mandatory for foreign female tourists in public places, though modest dress is strictly enforced at religious landmarks). Note that entry to the holy city of Mecca and the central Prophet's Mosque courtyard in Medina is restricted exclusively to Muslims. Alcohol is strictly prohibited across the entire kingdom.

## Category: Safety And Scams
Saudi Arabia is one of the safest countries in the world with extremely low crime rates and zero tolerance for theft or public disturbances. Street crime and tourist scams are virtually non-existent. The primary precautions involve avoiding outdoor desert activities during extreme midday summer heat (temperatures can exceed 45°C) and ensuring you only embark on desert dune bashing and stargazing excursions in the AlUla or Empty Quarter deserts with licensed tour operators and certified 4x4 guides.
