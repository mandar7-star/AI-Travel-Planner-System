# Destination: Czech Republic

## Category: Visa
Indian passport holders require a Schengen Short-Stay Visa to visit the Czech Republic (Czechia). Applications are processed through VFS Global Czech Republic in India with required documentation including passport with six months validity, 30,000 EUR travel medical insurance, hotel booking vouchers across Prague and other regions, return flight bookings, bank statements showing sufficient financial solvency, and tax returns. The visa processing time is standard at 15 working days.

## Category: Transport
Prague possesses an exceptionally integrated and affordable public transit network operated by DPP, featuring three underground metro lines (A, B, C), vintage trams, and buses. Buy a 24-hour or 72-hour transit pass and remember to validate it at the yellow stamping machines upon entering metro stations or vehicles. For intercity journeys to destinations like Český Krumlov, Brno, or Karlovy Vary, České dráhy (national rail) and RegioJet private trains and coaches offer comfortable journeys. Uber and Bolt operate reliably throughout Prague.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The official currency is the Czech Koruna (CZK / Kč), though some tourist shops display prices in Euros at unfavourable exchange rates. Contactless card payments (Visa, Mastercard) are accepted in virtually all Prague establishments, restaurants, supermarkets, and metro ticket machines. Carry a small amount of CZK cash for traditional beer pubs (hospoda), public restrooms, and small souvenir kiosks. Never exchange currency with strangers on the street offering zero-commission exchanges, as they give worthless obsolete banknotes.

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
Czech culture values modesty, punctuality, and quiet consideration in public places. When entering someone's home, always remove your outdoor footwear and switch to guest slippers provided by the host. When dining in traditional pubs, it is common practice to share large wooden tables with strangers if spare seats are available; a polite 'Je tu volno?' (Is this seat free?) is customary before sitting down.

## Category: Safety And Scams
The Czech Republic is among the safest countries in the world, but central Prague (Charles Bridge, Old Town Square, and Astronomical Clock) has active pickpocketing rings during crowded spectacles. Avoid standalone ATM machines (especially Euronet) offering inflated dynamic currency conversion rates, and stick to major bank ATMs like Česká spořitelna or Komerční banka. Never take unmetered street taxis flagged down at railway stations or tourist squares; use Uber, Bolt, or AAA Taxi.
