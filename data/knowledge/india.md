# Destination: India

## Category: Transport
Domestic travel across India is powered by the extensive Indian Railways (IRCTC) network and a robust domestic flight grid. For journeys under 500 km, Vande Bharat Express and Shatabdi trains provide high-speed chair car options. For long-distance intercity routes, 3-tier AC (3AC), 2-tier AC (2AC), or First Class AC (1AC) provide comfortable overnight sleeper travel. Book train tickets in advance via IRCTC (irctc.co.in). For short hops within cities, app-based cabs (Ola, Uber) and auto-rickshaws (using meters or prepaid counters) are prevalent.

## Category: Budgeting and Currency
The currency is the Indian Rupee (INR ₹). India is predominantly a digital payment economy via UPI (Unified Payments Interface). International travelers can access UPI for foreigners via specific partner apps (e.g., Cheq, Thomas Cook) at airports. Always keep some cash (INR 100 and 500 notes) for rural vendors and local tips.

## Category: Culture and Etiquette
Dress modestly when visiting religious temples, mosques, gurudwaras, and heritage sites (cover shoulders and knees, and cover head in gurudwaras). Remove footwear outside religious sanctums. Using the right hand for eating and giving/receiving items is customary.
