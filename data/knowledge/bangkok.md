# Destination: Bangkok

## Category: Visa
Thailand offers visa exemptions or 60-day tourist visa entries for Indian passport holders under bilateral travel facilitation policies (check current periodic waiver announcements, or obtain 15-day Visa on Arrival / 60-day e-Visa). Ensure you have proof of funds (minimum 10,000 THB per person or 20,000 THB per family in cash or bank balance), confirmed hotel bookings, and a return flight within the permitted stay duration.

## Category: Transport
Bangkok traffic is notoriously congested. Rely on the elevated BTS Skytrain and underground MRT lines to bypass traffic. For riverfront attractions (Wat Arun, Grand Palace, ICONSIAM), use the Chao Phraya Express Boat or tourist hopper ferries. For point-to-point taxis, use the Grab or Bolt app to lock in fares and avoid meter disputes. Tuk-tuks should be negotiated in advance and used primarily for short scenic hops.

## Category: Culture and Etiquette
The Thai Royal Family and Buddhist traditions are revered. Showing disrespect to images or statues of the King or the Buddha is illegal (lèse-majesté). Remove shoes before entering Buddhist temples and Thai homes. Dress conservatively when visiting the Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew (shoulders and knees covered, no ripped jeans or sleeveless tops). The traditional greeting is the 'Wai' (pressing palms together with a slight bow).
