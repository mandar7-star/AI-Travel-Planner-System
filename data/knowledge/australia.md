# Destination: Australia

## Category: Visa
Indian passport holders require a Visitor Visa (Subclass 600 - Tourist Stream) to travel to Australia. Applications are submitted 100% online through the official ImmiAccount portal on the Department of Home Affairs website (immi.homeaffairs.gov.au). The visa application fee is approximately 190 AUD, with processing times generally ranging from 15 to 30 days depending on seasonal volume. Requirements include a valid passport, detailed day-wise itinerary, proof of accommodation and funds (6-month bank statements, ITR), and evidence of ties to India (employment letter/business registration).

## Category: Transport
Australia is an expansive continent where domestic flights on Qantas, Virgin Australia, and Jetstar are essential for traveling between major cities like Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Cairns, and Perth. In Sydney, the Opal card transit network (or any contactless credit/debit card) seamlessly accesses trains, the famous Sydney Ferries from Circular Quay, light rail, and buses. In Melbourne, the Free Tram Zone in the central business district (CBD) allows free travel across downtown landmarks. Renting a car is the top choice for iconic road journeys such as the Great Ocean Road.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The currency is the Australian Dollar (AUD / $). Australia is an almost completely cashless society with contactless tap-and-go card payments (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay) accepted universally for everything from public transport to flat whites at neighborhood cafes. Carrying physical AUD cash is almost never required, except occasionally at select rural open-air weekend markets. Unlike North America, tipping is not expected or mandatory in Australia, though leaving 5-10% for exceptional dining service is appreciated.

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
Australian culture is casual, friendly, egalitarian, and rooted in outdoor lifestyle and sports ('mateship'). Greetings are informal, such as 'G'day' or 'How are you going?'. Always swim strictly between the red and yellow flags on surf beaches (such as Bondi or Manly), as trained lifeguards actively monitor these safe zones for dangerous rip currents. Remember that Australia has strictly enforced biosecurity border laws: declare all food items, seeds, plant materials, and wooden souvenirs on your Incoming Passenger Card upon arrival.

## Category: Safety And Scams
Australia is one of the safest countries in the world with very low crime rates and high living standards. The main safety hazards relate to wildlife and natural elements: use high-SPF sunscreen to prevent sunburn under the strong Australian sun, stay alert for jellyfish (stingers) in northern tropical Queensland (Cairns) during stinger season, and never swim in estuaries or rivers in the Northern Territory or North Queensland due to saltwater crocodiles.
