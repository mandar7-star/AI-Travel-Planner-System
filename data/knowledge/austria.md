# Destination: Austria

## Category: Visa
Indian citizens require a Schengen Short-Stay Visa to visit Austria for tourism. Applications should be submitted through VFS Global Austria in India with mandatory documentation including passport with six months validity, Schengen travel medical insurance covering minimum 30,000 EUR, confirmed flight itineraries, hotel vouchers across Vienna, Salzburg, and Tyrol, past 3-6 months bank statements, and income tax documents. The standard Schengen visa processing time is approximately 15 working days.

## Category: Transport
Austria has a world-class public transport network managed by ÖBB (Austrian Federal Railways), with high-speed Railjet trains connecting Vienna to Salzburg, Innsbruck, Graz, and neighboring countries like Germany, Switzerland, and Hungary. In Vienna, the Wiener Linien network includes U-Bahn subways, trams (Strassenbahn), and buses that operate on an honor-based ticket system; always purchase and validate tickets before riding, as plainclothes inspectors conduct random checks with hefty fines.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The official currency is the Euro (EUR). Contactless credit and debit cards (Visa/Mastercard) are widely accepted across hotels, major restaurants, grocery stores, and rail ticket counters. However, similar to Germany, Austria maintains a strong cultural affinity for cash; traditional Viennese coffeehouses (Kaffeehäuser), local Heuriger wine taverns, small mountain chalets, and sausage stands (Würstelstand) often require cash payment. Always carry 20 to 50 EUR in cash.

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
Austrian culture values formal politeness, cultural refinement, and punctuality. When greeting people, say 'Grüß Gott' (in Austria/Bavaria) or 'Guten Tag', and make direct eye contact when clinking glasses with a toast of 'Prost!'. In traditional Viennese coffeehouses, coffee is served on a small silver tray accompanied by a glass of water, meant to be sipped leisurely without rushing. Sunday is a day of rest with retail shops and supermarkets closed.

## Category: Safety And Scams
Austria is consistently ranked as one of the safest countries in the world. Basic precautions against pickpockets should be exercised in crowded tourist zones such as Vienna's Stephansplatz, around Salzburg's Mirabell Palace, and at main railway stations (Wien Hauptbahnhof). Beware of street ticket touts dressed in Mozart period costumes near the Vienna Opera selling overpriced classical music concerts with poor seat views.
