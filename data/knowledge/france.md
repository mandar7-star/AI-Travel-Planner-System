# Destination: France

## Category: Visa
Indian passport holders require a Schengen Visa to visit Paris and France. Apply through VFS Global for France at least 30 to 90 days before your travel date. Mandatory requirements include travel medical insurance with minimum coverage of 30,000 EUR, confirmed return flight tickets, proof of accommodation across all Schengen states, a detailed day-wise itinerary, 3 months bank statements certified by the bank, 3 years ITR, and employment leave approval letters. Processing time is usually 15 working days.

## Category: Transport
Paris has an extensive public transportation network run by RATP. The Paris Metro and RER suburban express trains are the fastest way to get between monuments, airports (CDG/Orly), and Disneyland Paris. Buy a Navigo Easy pass or a Paris Visite travel pass for unlimited zones 1-3 or zones 1-5 travel. TGV high-speed trains connect Paris to Nice, Lyon, Bordeaux, and neighboring European countries in 2 to 4 hours.

## Category: Culture and Etiquette
When entering any shop, bakery, or cafe in France, greet the staff with 'Bonjour Madame/Monsieur' and say 'Merci, Au revoir' when leaving. Failure to do so is considered rude. Dress smartly casual; avoid overly casual activewear in restaurants. Service charge is included by law in cafe and restaurant bills ('service compris'), but leaving a small tip (1-2 EUR for drinks, 5-10% for exceptional dining) is appreciated.

## Category: Safety and Scams
Beware of pickpockets in crowded tourist hubs (Eiffel Tower, Louvre, Montmartre, Gare du Nord) and on Metro Line 1 and Line 4. Ignore street petitions, gold ring scams, and string bracelet vendors around Sacre-Coeur. Keep valuables inside zippered bags worn across the front of your body.
