# Destination: Germany

## Category: Visa
Indian passport holders require a Schengen Short-Stay Visa to visit Germany for tourism. Applications are processed through VFS Global Germany in India and should be booked several weeks in advance due to high appointment demand. The application requires a valid passport, comprehensive Schengen travel medical insurance of minimum 30,000 EUR, detailed day-by-day travel plan, proof of accommodation, round-trip flight reservations, 3-6 months bank statements, and income tax documents. Standard processing time takes approximately 15 working days.

## Category: Transport
Germany is famous for its punctual and extensive railway network operated by Deutsche Bahn (DB), with high-speed ICE (Intercity Express) trains connecting all major cities and neighboring nations. In major metropolitan areas like Berlin, Munich, and Frankfurt, public transit integrates the U-Bahn (underground metro), S-Bahn (suburban rail), trams, and buses. Buy single, day, or multi-day tickets and always remember to validate them at platform punch boxes before boarding. For scenic road trips along the Romantic Road or Black Forest, renting a car gives access to the famous Autobahn highway system.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The currency is the Euro (EUR). While major department stores, railway stations, supermarkets, and hotels accept Visa and Mastercard contactless cards, Germany remains surprisingly cash-friendly. Many traditional bakeries, beer gardens, kiosks (Spätis), and small restaurants operate on a cash-only ("Nur Bares") basis. Always keep 20 to 50 EUR cash and some 50-cent/1-euro coins for paid public restrooms (Sanifair).

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
German culture values punctuality, efficiency, direct communication, and strict adherence to social rules. Avoid jaywalking, even at empty street crossings with red pedestrian signals, as it is viewed as irresponsible and socially frowned upon. Keep noise levels down on Sundays (Ruhetag / quiet day), when most retail shops and supermarkets remain closed. In restaurants, tap water is not routinely served for free; specify 'stilles Wasser' for still bottled water or 'mit Kohlensäure' for sparkling.

## Category: Safety And Scams
Germany is a very safe travel destination with robust public policing. Basic precautions against pickpockets are advised around major railway hubs (Hauptbahnhof) in Berlin, Frankfurt, and Cologne, and during crowded festivals like Oktoberfest in Munich. Beware of fake charity petitions near tourist landmarks and unmetered independent taxis. Always check train schedules on the DB Navigator app for live track change and delay notifications.
