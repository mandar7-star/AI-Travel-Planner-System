# Destination: General

## Category: Travel Insurance
Comprehensive international travel insurance is essential for overseas travel and mandatory for Schengen and several Asian visa applications. Ensure policies cover emergency medical evacuation (minimum $50,000 to $100,000 coverage), trip cancellation/interruption, loss of passport, baggage delay, and extreme adventure sports if applicable.

## Category: Packing and Electronics
Carry a universal travel adapter supporting types C, G, D, and A plug standards. Pack a certified power bank (under 100Wh to meet airline cabin carry-on regulations). Keep physical and digital cloud copies of passport biographical pages, active visas, hotel bookings, and travel insurance policy numbers.

## Category: Flight and Accommodation Booking Strategy
Book international flights 6 to 10 weeks prior to departure for optimal pricing. Use price tracking alerts on Google Flights and Skyscanner. When booking hotels, look for refundable rates with free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in to maintain flexibility.
