# Destination: Qatar

## Category: Visa
Indian citizens traveling to Qatar can obtain a free 30-day Visa on Arrival (extendable for an additional 30 days) at Hamad International Airport (DOH). Key conditions for Indian travelers include a passport valid for at least six months, a confirmed return flight ticket, and a mandatory confirmed hotel reservation booked exclusively through the official Discover Qatar website for the entire duration of the stay. Additionally, travelers should ensure adequate proof of funds or valid credit card availability upon entry.

## Category: Transport
Doha has one of the world's most modern and comfortable transit networks, centered on the driverless Doha Metro system comprising the Red, Gold, and Green lines connecting the airport, Katara Cultural Village, Souq Waqif, and Lusail. Purchase a Standard Travel Card or Gold Club Card at any metro station for inexpensive contactless travel. Karwa metered taxis, Uber, and Careem operate extensively with clean, air-conditioned vehicles across greater Doha. For scenic waterfront trips along the Corniche, traditional wooden dhow cruises provide picturesque skyline views.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The currency is the Qatari Riyal (QAR), which is pegged to the US Dollar at a fixed rate of approximately 3.64 QAR per USD. Qatar is overwhelmingly a digital economy with international contactless credit and debit cards (Visa/Mastercard) accepted almost universally across malls, museum counters, taxis, and restaurants. Carrying a small amount of QAR cash (20 to 50 QAR) is useful for small purchases and bargaining at traditional market stalls inside Souq Waqif.

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
Qatari culture is based on Arab hospitality combined with traditional Islamic values. Modest dress is expected in public areas like shopping malls, cultural complexes, and government buildings, requiring shoulders and knees to be covered for both men and women. Avoid public displays of affection (PDA), and ask permission before taking photos of local Qatari nationals, especially women. Alcohol is strictly regulated and served only in licensed 4-star and 5-star international hotel restaurants and bars.

## Category: Safety And Scams
Qatar is consistently ranked as one of the safest countries in the world with virtually zero violent crime or street pickpocketing. Safety risks are largely related to extreme desert heat and intense sun exposure during the summer months (June to September), when outdoor walking should be restricted to early morning or late evening hours. Desert safari dune bashing tours should only be booked through licensed safari operators with certified 4x4 drivers and roll-caged vehicles.
