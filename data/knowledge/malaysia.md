# Destination: Malaysia

## Category: Visa
Indian citizens enjoy visa-free entry to Malaysia for stays of up to 30 days for tourism purposes. Travelers must complete the mandatory Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC) online within three days prior to arrival. Key entry requirements include a passport valid for at least six months, confirmed return or onward flight tickets, and proof of hotel bookings and sufficient funds.

## Category: Transport
Kuala Lumpur and surrounding regions feature a modern transit network comprising the LRT, MRT, Monorail, and KLIA Ekspres train connecting the international airport to KL Sentral in 28 minutes. Purchase a Touch 'n Go card for seamless contactless payments across all transit lines and toll plazas. Grab is the premier ride-hailing app in Malaysia and is inexpensive compared to flagged street taxis. Long-distance express buses and KTMB intercity ETS trains connect KL to Penang, Ipoh, and Melaka efficiently.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The currency is the Malaysian Ringgit (MYR). International Visa and Mastercard credit and debit cards are widely accepted at shopping malls, hotels, and established restaurants. Small food stalls, night markets (Pasar Malam), and local Mamak eateries typically require cash, so withdraw MYR from major local ATMs like Maybank or CIMB. Cross-border QR payment linkages connecting UPI and DuitNow QR are increasingly being rolled out across merchants.

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
Malaysia is a diverse Muslim-majority nation with Chinese, Indian, and indigenous communities living in harmony. Remove your shoes before entering homes, mosques, and Hindu or Buddhist temples. Dress modestly when visiting sacred sites, such as the Batu Caves or the National Mosque, with shoulders and knees covered. When greeting or eating with locals, use the right hand, as the left hand is traditionally considered unclean.

## Category: Safety And Scams
Malaysia is generally safe for tourists, though caution should be exercised regarding bag snatching by motorists in crowded urban areas. Always walk facing oncoming traffic and hold bags away from the curbside. Avoid unmetered taxis outside major transit hubs and rely on Grab for predictable fares. Be cautious of unofficial ticket vendors near popular tourist attractions in Kuala Lumpur and Penang.
