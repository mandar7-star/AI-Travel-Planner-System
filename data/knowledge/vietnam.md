# Destination: Vietnam

## Category: Visa
Indian passport holders require an electronic visa (e-Visa) to enter Vietnam for tourism. The e-Visa can be applied for directly through the official Vietnam Immigration portal and is available as a 30-day or 90-day single or multiple-entry visa. The official fee is 25 USD for single-entry and 50 USD for multiple-entry, with standard processing taking 3 to 5 business days. Ensure passport details match your airline tickets exactly to prevent entry issues at the border.

## Category: Transport
Getting around Vietnam is easy using the Grab and Be apps for affordable car and motorbike taxi rides in major cities like Hanoi, Da Nang, and Ho Chi Minh City. For intercity travel, domestic flights on Vietnam Airlines, VietJet, or Bamboo Airways are frequent and budget-friendly. Sleeper buses and scenic trains (such as the Reunification Express connecting Hanoi to Hue and Da Nang) provide economical overland connections. Crossing busy city roads requires walking at a slow, predictable pace so motorbikes can navigate smoothly around you.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The currency is the Vietnamese Dong (VND), and cash remains the most practical payment method for street food, local markets, and small cafes. International credit cards are accepted in major hotels, modern shopping centers, and upscale restaurants, often with a 2-3% bank processing surcharge. High-denomination VND banknotes look similar, so verify the zeros carefully before handing over cash. ATMs from banks like VPBank, BIDV, and Vietcombank are widely available for cash withdrawals.

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
Vietnamese culture is deeply rooted in respect, family hierarchy, and Buddhist traditions. Dress modestly with shoulders and knees covered when visiting pagodas, temples, and historical monuments like the Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum. Remove shoes when entering private homes or sacred areas. It is customary to hand items or money with both hands as a sign of respect, and public displays of anger should be avoided.

## Category: Safety And Scams
Vietnam is a safe travel destination with low violent crime rates. The most common tourist concerns are overcharging by unmetered street taxis and petty drive-by phone or bag snatching in Ho Chi Minh City. Stick to Grab or reputable metered taxi companies like Mai Linh and Vinasun. Agree on prices in writing beforehand when taking cycle rickshaws (cyclos) or hiring private boats in Halong Bay and Ninh Binh.
