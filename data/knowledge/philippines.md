# Destination: Philippines

## Category: Visa
Indian passport holders require a tourist visa (9a) to visit the Philippines, which can be applied for through the Philippine Embassy/Consulates in India or via the Philippine e-Visa portal where available. Indian citizens holding a valid, current visa or permanent residency from the US, Japan, Australia, Canada, Schengen Area, UK, or Singapore can enter the Philippines visa-free for an initial stay of up to 14 days (extendable up to 21 days). All arriving passengers must fill out the online eTravel portal declaration within 72 hours before arrival.

## Category: Transport
Travelling between the 7,000+ islands of the Philippines requires domestic flights with carriers like Philippine Airlines, Cebu Pacific, or AirAsia, or inter-island Ro-Ro ferries and fast-craft catamarans (OceanJet). In urban hubs like Manila and Cebu, ride-hailing app Grab is the safest and most reliable transportation option. For authentic local experiences over short distances, colorful Jeepneys and motorized tricycles are commonplace; agree on the fare beforehand or pay the standard municipal flat rate.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The currency is the Philippine Peso (PHP). Major hotels, upscale shopping centers, and diving resorts in destinations like Boracay and Manila accept international Visa and Mastercard credit cards. Cash is indispensable when exploring island destinations such as El Nido, Coron, and Siargao, where power outages and offline card terminals are frequent. ATMs are found in major towns (BPI, BDO, Metrobank), but carry sufficient backup cash when heading to remote islands.

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
Filipino culture is warm, family-oriented, and renowned for genuine hospitality with deep Christian traditions. The respectful greeting gesture 'Mano' (taking an elder's hand and placing it gently against one's forehead) is used for senior family members, and addressing elders with 'Po' and 'Opo' indicates polite respect. English is widely spoken and serves as one of the country's official languages, making communication effortless. Dress modestly when visiting historical cathedrals like San Agustin Church in Intramuros.

## Category: Safety And Scams
The main tourist areas in the Philippines (Boracay, Palawan, Bohol, Cebu) are friendly and hospitable. Watch out for taxi drivers at Manila Airport refusing to turn on meters; always use the official coupon taxi booths or book via Grab. Exercise standard street awareness against pickpockets in crowded public markets, and verify that tour boat operators in island-hopping regions are accredited by the Department of Tourism (DOT).
