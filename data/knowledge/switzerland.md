# Destination: Switzerland

## Category: Visa
Indian citizens require a Schengen Short-Stay Visa to travel to Switzerland for tourism. Applications should be lodged through VFS Global for Switzerland with proof of travel itinerary, confirmed hotel bookings, valid return flights, minimum 30,000 EUR travel medical insurance, 3-6 months bank statements, and income tax documents. Switzerland experiences very high visa application volumes during the spring and summer holiday seasons, so applying 2 to 3 months in advance is strongly recommended. The standard visa processing time is roughly 15 working days.

## Category: Transport
Switzerland features the world's most integrated and scenic public transport network, operated by SBB and regional operators. The Swiss Travel Pass is the premier option for international visitors, granting unlimited travel on all national trains, panoramic routes (like the Glacier Express and Bernina Express), post buses, lake boats, and city transit, plus free entry to over 500 museums. High-mountain excursions (Jungfraujoch, Matterhorn Glacier Paradise, Mount Titlis) operate like clockwork via cable cars and cogwheel railways, offering pass holder discounts.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The official currency is the Swiss Franc (CHF), though Euros are occasionally accepted at major tourist shops near borders with change returned in CHF. Credit and debit cards (Visa/Mastercard) are accepted across almost all stores, mountain huts, ticket counters, and restaurants. While cashless payments are seamless, keeping 20 to 50 CHF cash is helpful for remote alpine dairy farms and self-service honor-system roadside farm stalls.

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
Swiss society values tranquility, environmental cleanliness, and punctuality. Trains and buses depart precisely to the minute, so always arrive at platforms a few minutes early. Respect quiet hours (Ruhezeit) after 10:00 PM in residential and hotel neighborhoods. Tap water from public fountains in towns and villages is mountain-fresh, clean, and completely safe to drink unless marked 'Nicht Trinkwasser'.

## Category: Safety And Scams
Switzerland is among the safest countries in the world with virtually negligible crime rates. Basic care of personal belongings should be maintained at bustling transport hubs like Zurich Hauptbahnhof and Geneva Cornavin. Alpine mountain safety is critical: check mountain weather webcams before ascending peaks, wear sturdy hiking boots on trails, and never hike off marked paths in alpine terrain.
