# Destination: Japan

## Category: Visa
Japan visa for Indian citizens requires a standard tourist visa (single or multiple entry). Applicants must apply through VFS Global or Japanese Consulates in India with a valid passport, flight itinerary, hotel booking confirmation, income tax returns (ITR), and recent 6-month bank statements showing sufficient funds (approx. INR 1.5 - 2 Lakhs minimum). Processing takes 5-7 working days. The visa fee itself is waived or minimal, with standard VFS processing service charges. Electronic visas (eVisa) may also be available for specific short-stay single-entry categories.

## Category: Transport
Japan's public transit is among the world's most punctual. The Shinkansen (Bullet Train) connects major hubs like Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, and Hiroshima. For long-distance intercity travel across regions, evaluate if the nationwide JR Pass or regional JR passes (like JR West or JR Kansai) offer better value based on your exact itinerary. For city travel inside Tokyo and Osaka, buy a digital or physical IC Card (Suica, Pasmo, or ICOCA) which works seamlessly on all metro, bus, and suburban train lines, as well as vending machines and convenience stores.

## Category: Currency and Payments
The currency is the Japanese Yen (JPY). While credit cards (Visa/Mastercard) are increasingly accepted in major department stores and chain hotels, cash remains essential for traditional ramen shops, temple entrance fees, street vendors, and coin lockers. Withdraw cash easily with foreign debit cards from 7-Bank ATMs located in 7-Eleven stores or Japan Post ATMs with low foreign exchange markups.

## Category: Culture and Etiquette
Respectful behavior is deeply valued. Always remove your shoes when entering homes, ryokans (traditional inns), and certain temple dining areas (look for genkan step-downs). Bowing slightly is the customary greeting. Tipping is not customary and can be perceived as insulting or confusing. Do not speak loudly on public transit, and place mobile phones on 'manner mode'. Avoid eating or drinking while walking down crowded streets; consume food near the shop where it was purchased.

## Category: Best Time and Weather
Spring (late March to mid-April) is famous for Cherry Blossoms (Sakura), while Autumn (October to November) features vibrant koyo autumn foliage and crisp weather. Avoid Golden Week (late April to early May) and Obon (mid-August) due to extreme domestic crowding and surging hotel rates. Summers (July-August) can be hot and humid, while winters offer world-class skiing in Hokkaido and Nagano.
