# Destination: Dubai

## Category: Visa
Indian passport holders with a valid US visa (minimum 6 months validity), US green card, UK residence permit, or EU residence permit are eligible for a 14-day Visa on Arrival (extendable once for 14 days) at Dubai and UAE ports for approx. AED 120-140. All other Indian passport holders must obtain a pre-arranged 30-day or 60-day tourist visa, easily processed through Emirates airlines, flydubai, travel agencies, or GDRFA/ICP official portals within 3-4 working days.

## Category: Transport
The Dubai Metro is fully automated, air-conditioned, and spans the Red Line (connecting DXB Airport, Sheikh Zayed Road, Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall, Dubai Marina) and Green Line (Old Dubai, Deira, Gold Souk). Purchase a Silver Nol Card for contactless tap-on/tap-off. Ride-hailing apps like Careem and Uber operate extensively, along with metered Dubai Taxis (DTC). For water transport across Dubai Creek, traditional wooden Abra boats cost only 1 AED per trip.

## Category: Culture and Laws
Dubai is modern but adheres to Islamic customs. Dress respectfully in public areas such as shopping malls, government offices, and cultural sites (shoulders and knees covered). Public displays of affection (PDA) should be avoided. Alcohol is permitted only in licensed hotels, restaurants, and lounges; drinking or being intoxicated in public streets is strictly illegal. During the holy month of Ramadan, be mindful of eating and drinking restrictions in open public areas during daylight fasting hours.
