# Destination: Greece

## Category: Visa
Indian passport holders require a Schengen Short-Stay Visa to visit Greece for tourism. Applications must be submitted through GVCW (Global Visa Center World) or VFS Global in India with proof of flight reservations, confirmed accommodation bookings across mainland Greece and island destinations, comprehensive 30,000 EUR Schengen travel insurance, 3-6 months bank statements, and income tax returns. Visa processing takes approximately 15 working days, with peak summer months experiencing elevated processing queues.

## Category: Transport
In Athens, the metro, tram, and suburban railway network connect Athens International Airport, Syntagma Square, the Acropolis, and the port of Piraeus seamlessly. Traveling between Greek islands (such as Santorini, Mykonos, Crete, and Naxos) is conducted via high-speed catamarans and conventional ferries operated by Blue Star Ferries, Seajets, and Hellenic Seaways, which should be booked weeks in advance during summer. Domestic flights on Aegean Airlines or Olympic Air offer fast hops between Athens and the islands.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The currency is the Euro (EUR). Electronic and contactless card payments are legally mandated and accepted at almost all retail stores, tavernas, car rentals, and archaeological sites across Greece. However, carrying some cash (20 to 50 EUR) is recommended for small rural island tavernas, beach sunbed rentals, and local port transfers. Official bank ATMs (such as Alpha Bank, Piraeus Bank, and Eurobank) offer fair exchange rates compared to standalone tourist ATMs (Euronet).

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
Greek culture is warm, family-centered, and renowned for 'Philoxenia' (hospitality towards strangers). When visiting Greek Orthodox monasteries (like those at Meteora), dress modestly by covering shoulders and knees; women may be required to wear long skirts, which are often provided at the entrance. The 'Moutza' hand gesture (extending an open palm towards someone's face) is an offensive insult and should be avoided. Dining is a relaxed, social affair that extends late into the evening.

## Category: Safety And Scams
Greece is a very safe country with low violent crime rates. In central Athens (Monastiraki, Omonia, and crowded metro trains to Piraeus port), be alert to pickpockets and distraction artists. At certain beach clubs or waterfront tavernas in Mykonos, verify seafood pricing (charged per 100g vs. per portion) in advance to avoid exorbitant surprise bills. Only take licensed yellow metered taxis in Athens, or hail them through the FREENOW app.
