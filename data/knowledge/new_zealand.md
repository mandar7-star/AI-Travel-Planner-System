# Destination: New Zealand

## Category: Visa
Indian passport holders require a Visitor Visa to travel to New Zealand for tourism. Applications must be submitted online via Immigration New Zealand's official portal (immigration.govt.nz). The visa fee is approximately 211 NZD (plus the mandatory International Visitor Conservation and Tourism Levy / IVL of 100 NZD). Processing typically takes between 4 to 6 weeks, so early application is essential. Mandatory documents include a passport with six months validity, proof of funds (at least 1,000 NZD per month of stay or 400 NZD per month if accommodation is prepaid), confirmed outward flight tickets, and travel insurance.

## Category: Transport
New Zealand is renowned worldwide for scenic road-tripping, making campervan or rental car hire the premier way to explore the North and South Islands (driving is on the left side of the road, identical to India). For inter-island travel between Wellington (North Island) and Picton (South Island), the Interislander and Bluebridge Cook Strait ferries provide scenic crossings through the Marlborough Sounds. Domestic flights operated by Air New Zealand and Jetstar connect major hubs like Auckland, Queenstown, Christchurch, and Rotorua in 1 to 2 hours.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The currency is the New Zealand Dollar (NZD / $). New Zealand is nearly 100% cashless, with EFTPOS, contactless credit and debit cards, and mobile payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay) accepted at virtually every business, from remote South Island petrol stations to Queenstown adventure sport desks. Physical cash is virtually unnecessary throughout your trip. Tipping is not part of New Zealand culture and is entirely voluntary, reserved only for truly outstanding fine-dining service.

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
New Zealand culture is a harmonious blend of indigenous Māori heritage ('Tikanga Māori') and European ('Pākehā') traditions, celebrated for the spirit of 'Manaakitanga' (hospitality and generosity). When visiting a traditional Māori Marae (meeting grounds), observe customary protocols such as participating in the 'Pōwhiri' welcome ceremony and removing shoes before entering the Wharenui. New Zealand enforces strict biosecurity quarantine controls at international airports: all hiking boots, outdoor gear, and food items must be declared upon arrival.

## Category: Safety And Scams
New Zealand is consistently ranked among the safest nations on Earth with virtually zero violent crime or tourist scams. The primary considerations are driving safety on winding alpine roads (where narrow single-lane bridges require yielding to oncoming traffic) and alpine weather volatility. When undertaking Great Walks or backcountry day hikes (like the Tongariro Alpine Crossing or Roys Peak), always check Department of Conservation (DOC) trail reports, carry layered thermal waterproof clothing, and register your hiking intentions.
