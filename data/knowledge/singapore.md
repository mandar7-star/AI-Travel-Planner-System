# Destination: Singapore

## Category: Visa
Indian passport holders require a tourist visa to enter Singapore, which must be applied for through authorized visa agents or strategic partners as Singapore does not offer direct online e-visa applications for individuals. Processing typically takes 3 to 5 business days, and the fee is approx. 30 SGD plus agent processing charges. Indian travellers with a valid US, UK, Schengen, or Australian visa can also utilize the 96-hour Visa-Free Transit Facility (VFTF) if transiting through Singapore Changi Airport to/from a third country. All visitors must submit the mandatory SG Arrival Card online within 3 days prior to arrival.

## Category: Transport
Singapore has one of the world's most efficient and accessible public transport systems, anchored by the MRT (Mass Rapid Transit) and comprehensive bus networks. Travellers can simply tap their contactless international credit or debit cards (Visa/Mastercard) directly at MRT gates and on buses without needing to purchase a physical EZ-Link transit card. Ride-hailing apps such as Grab, Gojek, and Tada are widely available and provide transparent metered pricing. Walking is safe and well-connected through covered walkways and underground pedestrian links.

## Category: Currency And Payments
The official currency is the Singapore Dollar (SGD). Digital and card payments are accepted almost universally across malls, supermarkets, taxis, and restaurants. However, cash is recommended for dining at traditional hawker centres, though many hawker stalls now accept digital payments. India's UPI payments are increasingly integrated with Singapore's PayNow network, allowing seamless QR code scanning with supported Indian banking apps at select merchant outlets.

## Category: Culture And Etiquette
Singapore is a multi-cultural society consisting of Chinese, Malay, Indian, and Eurasian communities with strict public civic standards. Littering, jaywalking, smoking outside designated zones, and eating or drinking on MRT trains and platforms carry strict fines. Chewing gum is prohibited and cannot be imported into the country. Modest attire is expected when visiting religious venues such as Sultan Mosque, Sri Mariamman Temple, or Buddha Tooth Relic Temple.

## Category: Safety And Scams
Singapore is consistently rated as one of the safest destinations in the world, with violent crime and petty theft being exceedingly rare. Common travel issues are limited to unofficial touts near certain nightlife precincts or overpriced electronic shops in select older malls. Always verify rental agreement terms when hiring scooters or cycles in parks, and book tourist attractions via authorized ticketing platforms to avoid invalid vouchers.
